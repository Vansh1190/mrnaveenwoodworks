How we work

Four simple
steps.

You get a written quote and a fixed delivery date before we start. No hidden charges.

  1. 01

    Free visit & measurement

    Our team comes to your home, measures every wall and niche, and understands what you need. No charge for this.

  2. 02

    Free 3D design

    See your finished room in 3D before any work begins. Change colours, sizes or layouts until you are happy.

    Built & installed

    Cut in our workshop, fitted at your home by the same team that measured it. Neat work, dust controlled.

  4. 04

    Checked & handed over

    Every shutter, hinge and edge is checked and the space is cleaned before we hand it over to you.
