How we build — 1.0 The plan

Free visit, real measurements.

We come, measure every wall and niche, and see how your family lives in the room. The plan is drawn to your home — not to a catalogue size.

2.0 The cut

Cut on machines, in our workshop.

Boards are cut to the plan on proper machines and edge-banded the same day — not guessed with a hand saw inside your home.

3.0 Assembly

Panels built to fit first time.

Wall panels, carcasses and shutters are built — and test-fitted — in the shop before they ever leave it.

4.0 The fit

One team fits it, clean as it goes.

The same crew that measured fits everything at your home — drawers glided in, doors aligned, hardware tightened, dust swept.

5.0 Handover

Lights on. Room over.

Every shutter opened and closed, every edge checked, the room cleaned — then we show you how to look after it for years.
