The method

Four steps.
No surprises.

You get drawings, a fixed BOQ, and a handover date before we cut anything.

  1. 01

    Measure

    We visit with a laser and a notepad. Every niche, pipe and beam gets recorded. No tape-from-photograph guesses.

  2. 02

    Draw

    Layouts, elevations and a 3D walk-through you can actually change — before a single sheet is cut.

  3. 03

    Build

    Cut in the shop, joined on site. Bordered, edge-banded and squared by the same hands that measured it.

  4. 04

    Finish

    Sanded, PU-coated, aligned on a spirit level, then handed over clean enough to use that night.
